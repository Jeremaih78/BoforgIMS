# Stage 8 — Coordination, attention, jobs and factual reports

27 October 2026, Africa/Harare. Authorized by “implement next stage” and subsequent “continue” messages. This implements Stage 8 of the agreed playbook and preserves Stages 1–7. Local software verification is separate from production activation, operating ownership and employee productivity evidence.

## Delivered workflows

**Coordination** provides structured blocker and handoff records with context, requested action, explicit resolver, expected response, acknowledgement, resolution/cancellation, revision and an immutable event history. Raising either kind puts the original task in Waiting and closes its active timer through the existing Focus lifecycle. Receipt never transfers ownership or accepts completion. A resolved matching request can return the original task to Pending; foreign blocker reasons and other open requests are preserved. Actual reassignment remains the existing authorized task workflow.

Resolvers must currently be authorized to read the source task. A request grants no new team/company/task authority. Cross-department participation therefore requires existing explicit company access. Fresh ownership, company/team snapshots, current membership and capability are checked at creation, response, dispatch and display. Actor-local retry keys deduplicate identical creation; reusing a key for different work fails. Optimistic revisions retain a conflicting response for review. The requester can select no escalation or escalation of unacknowledged requests after the chosen expected response; acknowledgement stops that escalation policy while the underlying work remains tracked.

Task details and blocked Focus wrap-up link to this workflow. Active structured requests participate in shared dependency readiness even if a raw status edit clears `BLOCKED`; manual Focus, scheduling and operator execution cannot bypass them. Obsolete requests from a different owner/company/team do not constrain the new assignment.

**Notifications** have an explicit owner and canonical action link. Reading, acknowledging and resolving are distinct. A request alert can acknowledge receipt; it does not resolve the task. Quiet hours, configured work hours, leave/fixed commitments, active Focus and individual/hourly digest preferences determine delivery windows. Unknown work hours stay unknown and hold routine delivery until reviewed or explicitly opted out. Held records are inspectable in the center without creating an interrupt. Digests retain per-source evidence and reauthorize each child. Cancelled, superseded, terminal or inaccessible sources disappear from actionable lists immediately; reconciliation repairs their delivery records. No all-active-user broadcast is used.

**End day reporting** records versioned shared-fact submissions when the employee explicitly saves today's review with reporting enabled. It creates report work transactionally, without copying reflection fields or automatically submitting historical reviews. Teamless employees keep their private review and receive a clear no-reporting-team notice. Reports show current roster, missing submissions, authorized recorded arrival/corrections, accepted completions/outcomes, intentions/time blocks, blockers/resolver/age, deadline risks and candidate next actions. Proposed next actions are labelled for readiness/capacity review in Now. Arrival and timer duration are not staffed-hour or productivity evidence.

Actual team managers and explicit active company report recipients can read scoped reports. `can_receive_reports` grants no underlying Task access; report links are added only when the viewer can read that Task. Source ownership/scope, current and recorded team boundaries, accepted outcome status and current audience are rechecked. Historical versions remain readable while those source/audience boundaries remain valid; a new factual correction appends a version. Identical consecutive facts reuse the latest version; A → B → A produces a third version. Private edits and clock/age ticks do not manufacture corrections.

Report policy supports explicit timezone, reporting weekdays, per-submission versus cutoff consolidation, append-revision corrections, visible missing submissions and test email mode. These are unconfigured for real companies by default. Configured cutoff policies alone schedule a fallback; per-submission policies do not silently acquire a cutoff fallback. Retention fields record a policy choice but do not purge immutable evidence automatically.

## One job and delivery foundation

The repository has PostgreSQL/Django and optional Redis caching, with no existing Celery/RQ/Huey deployment. Stage 8 uses one PostgreSQL transactional outbox and management-command worker/scheduler, avoiding another broker or competing queue. See the [jobs runbook](TASKER_JOBS_RUNBOOK_2026-10-27.md) for exact commands, supervision and recovery contracts.

`OutboxJob` stores an allowlisted kind, unique dedupe key, bounded ID/provenance payload, content digest, attempts, due time, lease/fence, safe result and generic failure code. Claiming uses a short `skip_locked` transaction. Handlers run without holding the outbox row while acquiring source locks; their database effects and final lease fence commit atomically. An expired, stolen or superseded lease cannot commit stale database effects. Transient failures use bounded exponential retry; exhausted failures remain visible for authorized manual retry. A known pre-acceptance delivery failure commits its ledger and consumes an attempt; quiet-window deferral does not consume the failure budget. Ambiguous acceptance becomes `UNKNOWN` and is not blindly retried.

Allowlisted work kinds are `coordination.dispatch`, `notification.deliver`, `notification.digest`, `reports.build`, `reports.deliver` and `operator.refresh`. Stages 9/11 must extend this same foundation. Payloads and failure diagnostics contain no copied note bodies, provider credentials or raw exception text. Safe handler result maps have a bounded count/size.

Stage 7's synchronous invalidation remains authoritative and, when its dispatch flag is enabled, commits the revision and refresh request together. Operator refresh coalesces to one reusable row per owner. The helper locks/reads the canonical state so an old scheduler selection cannot overwrite a newer revision. A newer revision supersedes in-flight work and receives a fresh attempt budget. A failed unchanged revision remains failed until explicit retry or a new source change.

Reconciliation repairs missed dispatch/delivery/report/operator work and cancels obsolete sources. Nullable check timestamps rotate bounded batches; report policy team cursors prevent a large company's early teams from starving later teams. Explicit submissions of older dates are included, without inferring missing historical submissions. All sweeps and the worker can run once locally; loop/supervision deployment is a separate owner action.

One `NotificationDelivery` ledger is shared by in-app and email work. It distinguishes queued, held, delivered in app, accepted by the test adapter, provider accepted, failed, cancelled and unknown. Email uses `MockReportEmailAdapter` only; no SMTP/provider implementation or real recipient contact is activated. Company report policy and recipient quiet/focus/work-hour preferences are both checked before test acceptance. Real provider acceptance, delivery and reading are not inferred from a queued job or mock receipt. WhatsApp remains later work.

Operational health reports queue states, recent worker heartbeat, failed work and expired leases. The UI exposes scoped coordination/attention/report workflows; global job diagnostics/retry and raw evidence admin are restricted to active superusers. Routine managers use their scoped report and delivery statuses. Detail retention clears old completed payload/results in bounded batches while retaining compact dedupe tombstones; pending/failed work and immutable delivery/report evidence are preserved. This does not claim a bounded total row count or approved company retention policy.
