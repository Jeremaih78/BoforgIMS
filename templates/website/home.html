Still wondering about something?

Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
I've never printed before. Where do I start?+

Start with what you want to make, the materials you plan to use and your budget. Use our setup guide, then ask our team to confirm the right equipment, supplies and training for you.

What's included in a starter package?+

Open “What's in this setup” on a package to see its current equipment and supplies. Prices and availability come from our shop. Please confirm the training, setup assistance and delivery included before payment.

Can I order outside Harare?+

Tell us your town when you enquire. Our team will confirm delivery options, charges, collection arrangements and estimated timing for your order.

Which printing method is right for T-shirts?+

It depends on the fabric, colour, finish and quantity. Sublimation, heat transfer vinyl and DTF have different requirements. Share your planned products so our team can help check compatibility.

Can I buy supplies without a starter package?+

Yes. Browse the shop for individual machines, inks, paper, films, vinyl and printable blanks. Search for your machine or supply and confirm compatibility if you are unsure.
